# FAFLOW — Principal & Executive Manual

This manual outlines the institutional oversight features available to the **Principal** and Academic Executives (`Role.principal`).

---

## 1. Executive Role & Capabilities

The Principal role provides read-only executive visibility across all departments and academic programs:
- **Campus-Wide Substitution Rate**: Real-time percentage of covered vs uncovered teaching periods across all departments.
- **Cross-Department Coverage Analytics**: Monitor which departments provide or require inter-department substitution assistance.
- **Academic Calendar & Attendance Compliance**: Review semester progress, working days, and holiday schedules.
- **Credit Balance Distributions**: Audit faculty workload and credit equity across all engineering and science departments.

---

## 2. Principal Executive Dashboard (`/principal/dashboard`)

The Executive Dashboard consolidates campus operations into high-level metrics:

| Metric | Description | Benchmark Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Campus Coverage Rate** | Percentage of vacated teaching slots successfully covered by qualified faculty today. | 95%+ |
| **Active Departments** | Count of academic departments with published semester schedules. | All Active Units |
| **Total Substitutions (30 Days)** | Number of peer-to-peer substitutions completed in the trailing 30 days. | Real-time metric |
| **Emergency Coverage Ratio** | Percentage of same-day substitution requests resolved within the 2-hour window. | 90%+ |

---

## 3. Departmental Performance Comparisons

- **Department Coverage Table**: Compares coverage rates, active leave requests, and substitution volume by department.
- **Autonomous Engine Utilization**: Visualizes whether departments operate in `autonomous`, `assisted`, or `manual` mode.
- **Workload & Credit Health Audit**: Identifies faculty members with high credit balances or credit deficits for administrative workload rebalancing.
