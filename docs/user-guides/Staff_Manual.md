# FAFLOW — Laboratory & Operational Staff Manual

This manual provides operating procedures for **Laboratory Staff** (`Role.lab_staff`) and **Non-Teaching Staff** (`Role.non_teaching_staff`) accessing the FAFLOW Staff Portal.

---

## 1. Staff Portal Overview & Login

1. Navigate to **`http://localhost:5173/login`**.
2. Enter your institutional staff username/email and password.
3. On first login, complete your password setup.
4. You will land on your personalized **Staff Workspace Dashboard** (`/staff/dashboard`).

---

## 2. Staff Dashboard Features (`/staff/dashboard`)

- **Staff Profile Card**: Displays your Name, Designation, Department, and Specialization.
- **Assigned Laboratories**: Lists physical laboratory rooms under your custody and maintenance schedule.
- **Live Credit & Leave Status**: Displays your current leave credit balance, annual limit, and recent activity.
- **Upcoming Duties & Quick Actions**: Fast links to apply for leaves and view duty records.

---

## 3. Applying for Operational Leave (`/staff/leaves`)

### How to Apply:
1. Click **"+ Apply for Leave"** on the dashboard or leave portal.
2. Select your Leave Date(s) (e.g., Single Day or Multi-Day range).
3. Select your Session Type:
   - *Full Day* (`1.0 Day`)
   - *Forenoon Session (FN)* (`0.5 Day`)
   - *Afternoon Session (AN)* (`0.5 Day`)
4. Select the Leave Category:
   - *Casual Leave (CL)*
   - *Medical Leave (ML)*
   - *Earned Leave (EL)*
   - *On-Duty (OD)*
   - *Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)*
5. Provide your reason for absence and submit.
6. Your request is routed directly to your **Operational Manager** for approval.

---

## 4. Understanding the Staff Credit & Leave Ledger

Operational staff participate in an automated credit accounting ledger:

- **Annual Leave Quota**: Default `12.0 Days/Year` (or customized by your Manager).
- **Leave Deductions**: Approved full-day leaves deduct `−1.0 Day`; half-day sessions deduct `−0.5 Day`.
- **Duty Credits**: Managers award compensatory credit days (`+1.0 Day`) for authorized weekend duties, examination coordination, or lab maintenance sessions.
- **Ledger Statement**: View your complete chronological transaction history, reasons, and running balance in the **My Credit Ledger** drawer on `/staff/leaves`.
